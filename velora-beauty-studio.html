@velorabeautystudio

A small studio, with time for every client.

Velora was started by a makeup artist and a hairstylist who were tired of rushed appointments. We book fewer clients each day so nobody is hurried out of the chair.

You will find modern technique here, and a deep respect for the traditions that shape how Nigerian women dress for the biggest days of their lives, from the fold of a gele to the shade of a lip.

Three hours in the chair and I never once felt rushed. My gele sat perfectly until midnight.

Adaeze O., Ikoyi
